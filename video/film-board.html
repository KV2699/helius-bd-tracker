The shapeThe confession is the film

Most hackathon demos are a feature tour. This one is a confession with evidence, and that is the only reason a judge will remember it. The arc is: everyone guesses → we refuse to → here is the rule I earned in published research → I pointed it at myself and failed → here is the fix, measured → here is where NEAR actually is.

Beat 4 is the film. Everything before it is setup and everything after it is proof. If the edit has to lose time, take it from beats 2 and 6 — never from 4.

The animationWhere the process moves

grounding-animation.html — open it, screen-record it, it loops. 1920×1080, no dependencies, runs offline.

It animates the single mechanism the whole film turns on. The model's quote sits on the left; the page we fetched sits on the right. The quote is scanned word by word and the matching run lights up in the page as it is confirmed. Four real cases, in order:

  • Ellipsis Labs — admitted, span found at offset 0.
  • GSR Markets — admitted, a hiring signal from their own careers page.
  • Velocity Protocol — refused, the model invented a sentence worth paying for.
  • Velocity Protocol — refused, a paraphrase. This case passed 49% of the time until the audit.

The data is lifted from data/snapshot/scan.json. The comparison on screen is the comparison the pipeline runs.
